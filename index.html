Unit Symbol Value

Length, mass and time units use exact definitions where they exist (e.g. international inch = 25.4 mm, avoirdupois pound = 0.45359237 kg). Historical, regional and astronomical units use their commonly accepted standard values.

Current exchange rates are fetched live from open.er-api.com when you're online and fall back to a built-in approximate snapshot otherwise. Historical currencies convert through their official fixed rates into today's successor currency (e.g. 1 EUR = 1.95583 DEM, 1 TRY = 1,000,000 TRL). That is a legal equivalence, not an inflation adjustment. Ancient coinage such as the Roman denarius has no fixed legal link to any modern currency, so it isn't listed.
